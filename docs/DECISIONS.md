# Decisions log

| Date | Decision | Reason | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-01 | three.js r186 `three/webgpu` (not Babylon) | Native TSL node materials, WebGPU and WebGL2 from one codebase, instancing, a built-in post chain (GTAO, bloom, FXAA) and clustered lighting addon. The spike worked on the first day. | Babylon.js (heavier, fewer TSL-like shader tools) |
| 2026-10-01 | City terrain is flat (y=0) with a procedural beach slope; DEM deferred to the village and highway regions | Chennai's slice is 2–8 m above sea level, and SRTM's ±3–5 m noise (buildings and trees) is larger than the real relief, so it would make roads bumpy and wrong | Copernicus DEM everywhere |
| 2026-10-01 | Harness uses system Chrome (`--channel=chrome`) | Playwright Chromium's Dawn fails to load dxil.dll on this machine, so it falls back to WebGL2 | SwiftShader (very slow) |
| 2026-10-01 | Post chain: single-sample scene pass, then GTAO, bloom and FXAA | MSAA depth textures cannot be gathered by GTAO under WebGPU | MSAA without AO |
| 2026-10-01 | Crowd mid and far tiers are procedural low-poly bodies animated in the vertex shader (limb pivots, head wobble, raised hand), with 3 LODs (~900 / ~350 / ~60 tris) | 10k people in about 160 draw calls; an equivalent of VAT without an offline bake. The near tier will get Blender-built rigged humans. | VAT bake from Blender (Phase 3 upgrade path) |
| 2026-10-01 | Performance budgets are measured on the Intel UHD 620 dev laptop | That's the hardware available. The integrated-graphics target is 30 fps on Low; we estimate a mid-range discrete GPU is 6–8× faster. | — |
| 2026-10-01 | Fictional city bus operator "MNT" (Maanagara Naveena Transport) with MTC-like blue livery | The brief requires fictional brands while keeping a realistic look | Use MTC directly |
| 2026-10-01 | Fictional parties TMEK (lighthouse, maroon/teal) and ATM (palmyra, sky blue/white/yellow) | Checked against the TN party flags and symbols listed in STYLE_BIBLE §10 | — |
| 2026-10-01 | Chunk size 200 m; buildings bucketed by centroid; roads, areas and the coast are global in meta.json | A 3.2 × 3.1 km region has 176 chunks with about 36 buildings each, and roads are only 0.5 MB in total | Per-chunk road clipping |
| 2026-10-01 | Move development to a MacBook Air M5 (24 GB); drop the browser-only and 20-second load requirements; switch to a native engine, recommended **Godot 4** (pending owner confirmation) | The owner wants higher visual quality. Godot's text scenes, GDScript and headless CLI are the most editable and automatable for Claude, and Forward+ on Metal gives SDFGI, volumetrics and SSR. | Unity HDRP (higher ceiling, but GUID/YAML scenes and an editor-centric workflow), staying on three.js |
| 2026-10-01 | **Godot 4.7.2 stable** (Forward+, Jolt) confirmed by the owner; project in `godot/`, data packs moved to `godot/packs/` | Text scenes/scripts, headless CLI, Metal on the M5 | Unity HDRP |
| 2026-10-01 | Harness runs in a Linux cloud workspace with Xvfb + Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) when the Mac isn't scriptable | This session can move files on the Mac but cannot run commands there. Lavapipe renders the same Forward+ pipeline at ~1 fps; lighting matches closely, performance numbers are meaningless there and are measured on the Mac | Waiting for Mac shell access |
| 2026-10-01 | Materials use procedural **tileable textures generated by `tools/textures/gen.py`** (numpy, frequency-domain noise) instead of downloaded CC0 packs | Poly Haven / ambientCG are not reachable from the build workspace; generated textures are CC0 by construction and tunable | Poly Haven/ambientCG (can be swapped in later on the Mac) |
| 2026-10-01 | Façade windows and shops use **interior mapping** (parallax rooms behind the glass) in `facade.gdshader` | Gives real depth to thousands of windows without geometry; shops show stocked shelves, homes show furniture, lit at night | Flat dark glass (prototype) |
| 2026-10-01 | Ground under tanks is clipped per 10 m sub-tile (`Geometry2D.clip_polygons`) rather than 1 m cells | 1 m cells produced 6.4 M vertices; clipping gives 89 k | Ground mesh with holes (Godot's triangulator has no hole support) |
| 2026-10-01 | Vehicles use a custom **raycast vehicle** on `RigidBody3D` (spring-damper suspension, slip-based tyre forces with a friction circle) instead of `VehicleBody3D` | Needs 3-wheel autos (tippy, narrow track), 2-wheel bikes with a lean controller, and full control over Indian-traffic behaviour later | VehicleBody3D (4-wheel assumptions, little control) |
| 2026-10-01 | Fonts are full OFL TTFs from the google/fonts repo (`tools/fonts/fetch.py`) | The old web subsets were split by unicode-range; Godot shapes Tamil with HarfBuzz and needs whole fonts | — |
| 2026-10-01 | **OSM plot infill** (`tools/osm/infill.py`): procedural plots along every road side and in block interiors where OSM has no buildings | OSM covers only ~30% of Mylapore's buildings; empty lots made streets read as "boxes by the road". Generated buildings carry `g: 1` and are rebuilt idempotently | Leave OSM gaps; hand-place |
| 2026-10-01 | Temple architecture derived from OSM temple compounds (gopuram on the east edge, smaller one west for big temples, vimana at centroid, kaavi compound wall) | Data-driven, works for every Hindu temple in a pack; Kapaleeshwarar gets 7/5 tiers at 37/24 m | Hand-modelled landmark meshes (can override later) |
| 2026-10-01 | **Realistic humans from MPFB2 (CC0) built headlessly with Blender `bpy`**, rigged to `cmu_mb` and animated with retargeted **CMU mocap**; garments from MPFB helper geometry + body surface; re-shaded per instance in Godot | Real human anatomy and faces with no paid/AI asset services; one shared skeleton/animation library; per-instance shading gives hundreds of combinations from 18 meshes | Procedural low-poly bodies (prototype), Mixamo (licence), AI generation (forbidden by the brief) |
| 2026-10-01 | Retargeting = per-bone world orientation copy with rest-roll correction; root motion removed for locomotion loops; loop points found by minimum pose difference | Bone names already match (cmu_mb ↔ CMU BVH); robust to the T-pose vs A-pose rest difference | Blender constraint baking (needs UI context) |
| 2026-10-01 | Horizon LOD: whole region as per-chunk box meshes (hidden when the chunk streams in) + a synthetic 2.6 km ring of city blocks | Chennai continues to the horizon; cheap (one draw per chunk) | Far ring to the region edge only |
| 2026-10-02 | **Vehicles modelled procedurally in Blender** (`tools/vehicles/build_vehicles.py`): Catmull-Rom lofted shells whose window/pillar/trim regions fall on exact grid rows; booleans for wheel arches (`use_self`); separate `wheel_<i>` nodes | Smooth, curved, non-boxy bodies with clean glass lines, fully scriptable; per-instance colours via instance uniforms so 15 meshes cover the whole fleet | Prism extrusions (prototype), downloaded models (licences, style mismatch) |
| 2026-10-02 | **Traffic is kinematic** (IDM car-following on road polylines + lateral offsets for weaving), with per-road density quotas by rank; near tier = pooled bodies with colliders and skeletal riders, far tier = MultiMesh low-detail bodies with VAT riders | Hundreds of vehicles at low cost; Indian lane-less behaviour is easier to express as lateral offsets than with physics; player collisions still work | Physics vehicles for all traffic (too expensive), lane graph (too rigid for Chennai traffic) |
| 2026-10-02 | Riding poses built by analytic two-bone IK against the real vehicle geometry (`build_anims.py` RIDES) | Hands land on the actual grips, feet on pegs/floor; CMU has no riding mocap | Hand-keyed poses |
| 2026-10-02 | **MakeHuman CC0 system assets** (photo skins, card hair, brows, lashes, casual suits, shoes) fitted by MPFB; textures re-tinted in shaders (skin tone, hair colour, garment colour from luminance detail) | Photographic faces/skin and real hair cards are a big realism step; CC0; re-tinting keeps per-person variety | Procedural shells only |
